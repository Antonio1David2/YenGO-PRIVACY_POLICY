# Privacy Policy — YenGo

*Last updated: October 2026*

This Privacy Policy describes how the mobile application YenGo ("we", "our", or "the app") collects, uses, and protects user information when you use our application.

---

### 1. Information We DO NOT Collect
YenGo is designed with a strict "privacy-by-design" approach. The app:
- Does not require account creation, registration, or login.
- Does not request, store, or access your contact information, name, email address, phone number, or precise GPS location.
- Does not access your camera, microphone, photo library, or personal files on your device.

---

### 2. On-Device Local Data Storage
All usage preferences and app data are stored strictly locally on your device:
- Selected currencies and recently entered amounts.
- Custom tax-free and discount settings.
- Conversion history voluntarily saved by the user.
- Selected visual theme and display language.
- In-app purchase status (ad removal).

This data is never transmitted to our own servers nor shared with third parties other than the service providers outlined below.

---

### 3. Third-Party Services
To provide free currency exchange rates, monetization, and in-app purchases, the app integrates with trusted third-party services:

#### A. Google AdMob (Google LLC)
The free version of YenGo displays non-intrusive banner ads provided by Google AdMob. Google may collect and process:
- Device advertising identifiers (Google Advertising ID / GAID).
- Network diagnostic data, performance metrics, and crash logs related to ads.
- User interactions with advertisements.
- For more details on how Google processes data, please consult the Google Privacy Policy (https://policies.google.com/privacy) and How Google uses information from sites or apps (https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites).

#### B. Google Play Billing (Google LLC)
If you choose to purchase the "Remove Ads" option, the transaction is processed securely through the Google Play Store payment gateway. We do not receive, store, or process your credit card numbers, bank details, or billing information.

#### C. Exchange Rate Financial APIs (Frankfurter / FloatRates)
The app makes public HTTP requests to fetch the latest official currency exchange rates (ECB and central banks). These requests do not contain any personally identifiable information (PII).

---

### 4. Data Security
We are committed to safeguarding your information. The app uses industry-standard HTTPS/TLS encryption for all network communication (fetching exchange rates and serving ads).

---

### 5. Children's Privacy
Our application is not specifically directed to children under 13 years of age, and we do not knowingly collect personal data from children.

---

### 6. Your Rights (GDPR / CCPA)
Because we do not store personal data on remote servers, you maintain full control over your information:
- To delete all locally stored data (conversion history and preferences), you can clear the app's data in Android Settings > Apps > YenGo > Storage > Clear Data, or simply uninstall the app.
- If you purchased the ad removal feature, you can restore it at any time on any Android device linked to your Google Account using the "Restore Purchases" button in the app.

---

### 7. Changes to this Privacy Policy
We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect modifications in the app or applicable legal requirements. Any updates will be reflected on this page.

---

### 8. Contact Us
If you have any questions or feedback regarding this Privacy Policy, please contact us through the developer support email provided on the Google Play Store listing.
